About Me

I am a Mechatronics Engineer, proudly graduated from Celal Bayar University. Currently, I am advancing my academic journey with a master's degree in Software Engineering at Katip Çelebi University. Professionally, I serve as a Senior Product Development Engineer at Çeksan, where I focus on designing and implementing innovative, efficient, and sustainable solutions for industrial machinery—particularly road sweeping vehicles. My role involves everything from mechanical design and optimization to collaborating across disciplines to bring complex products from concept to reality. One of the milestones in my career has been contributing to a patent project that is now published on Google Patents, showcasing my commitment to innovation and problem-solving. Beyond mechanical engineering, I am deeply passionate about software development and UI/UX design. I'm actively working on improving my skills in Python, PyQt, and web technologies, building both engineering tools and creative personal projects. I enjoy merging mechanical systems with intelligent software to build smarter, more adaptive solutions. In every project I take on, I aim to bridge the gap between engineering and technology—with a constant desire to learn, adapt, and grow.
